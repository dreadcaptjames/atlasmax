TECHNICAL REFERENCE / 05 Data confidence & how optimization works +

This is a deterministic, goal-first layout solver. It assigns eligible supercharged slots by your mission profile, connects technology groups, then refines confirmed adjacency and fragmentation. It does not calculate exact DPS, range or hidden game multipliers, and does not claim a global optimum.

“Confirmed contact” means the dataset identifies the pair as a game adjacency relationship. Grouping reactors or habitation together is an organizational preference unless a specific relationship is confirmed. Procedural rolls vary. Use module preference to rank your strongest rolls within equal priority.

Source references appear in each module inspector. Unverified part mappings remain visible in the construction manifest and do not silently become fabricated technology. Match the generated inventory against your in-game equipment, particularly after updates.

FLIGHT MANUAL / QUICK START

Your build, in six steps.

  1. Choose equipment, variant, class and mission profile.
  2. Add installed technology. Corvette construction parts generate their corresponding modules.
  3. Set the grid to match your inventory. Block unavailable slots.
  4. Select Supercharge and mark the actual gold slots in your game. Fresh visits always start with none marked.
  5. Optimize. Inspect module details; move, swap or pin any module when you need a specific placement.
  6. Export a PDF report or save a JSON backup to resume later.

Preferences rank equal-priority modules; they are not fictional stat multipliers. Your goal always wins over those preferences. Use the in-game stats to verify the final result.
